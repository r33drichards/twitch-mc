Operate the piglin gold farm from where you are standing.

Fighting
- Hold the netherite sword and attack zombified piglins that are close enough to hit.
- Piglins only come to you once they are provoked. Each entity carries `aggressive`:
  true means its attack goal is running, false means it is ignoring you. `hostile`
  only means it belongs to a monster class, so a peaceful piglin still shows
  hostile true — judge by `aggressive`.
- If no piglin nearby is aggressive, provoke them: equip snowballs, face a piglin,
  and use the snowball. Throwing one at a piglin angers the group.
- If you have no snowballs, open a chest that is within reach and take a stack.

Processing
- Golden swords, golden helmets, chestplates, leggings and boots smelt down into
  gold nuggets. Open the furnace, move the gear in, add fuel if it needs some, and
  take the nuggets out once they are ready.
- Craft gold nuggets into gold ingots, and gold ingots into gold blocks, at the
  crafting table.
- Work at whatever is in reach; the stations list gives each one's distance, and
  anything out of reach needs you to move closer first.

Upkeep
- Eat rotten flesh when food is low. Eating needs the item held and the use held
  down, not a single click.
- Stay on the platform. There is a drop ahead of you; do not walk off it.

Finish when everything in the inventory is gold blocks.
