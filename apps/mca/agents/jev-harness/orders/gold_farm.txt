Run the piglin gold farm from where you stand, and turn everything into gold blocks.

Zombified piglins drop gold nuggets and golden gear when killed. Their gear smelts
into nuggets in the furnace; nuggets craft into ingots and ingots into blocks at the
crafting table. Snowballs are in the chests here, and a snowball thrown at a piglin
angers the whole group into coming for you. Eat when you get hungry.

Two things you cannot see for yourself: `aggressive` is the only sign a piglin is
actually coming for you, since `hostile` just means it belongs to a monster class;
and there is a drop off the edge of this platform.

Done when everything you carry is gold blocks.
