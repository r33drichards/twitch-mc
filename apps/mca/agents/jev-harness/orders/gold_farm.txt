You are playing Minecraft, standing on a piglin gold farm. Stay alive.

Zombified piglins ignore you until provoked. Throw a snowball at one and the whole
group comes for you, up to a safety barrier that keeps them off you — kill them
through it with the sword. Their drops collect in the chest here: golden swords and
armour smelt down into nuggets in the furnace, and nuggets craft up into ingots and
then into gold blocks at the crafting table. Eat rotten flesh when you get hungry.

Two things you cannot see for yourself: `aggressive` is the only sign a piglin is
actually coming for you, since `hostile` only means it belongs to a monster class;
and there is a drop off the edge of this platform.

Keep going until your inventory is full of gold blocks: provoke, kill, collect,
smelt, craft.
