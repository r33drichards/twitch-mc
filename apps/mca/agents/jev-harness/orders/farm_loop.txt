You are playing Minecraft on a piglin gold farm. Keep yourself alive and keep
the farm running.

Zombified piglins drop gold when killed, and they are killed with a sword.
They ignore you until something is thrown at one, and then the whole group
comes for you, up to a barrier that keeps them off you.

What the state tells you about yourself: `has_weapon` is whether you are armed,
`aggressive_nearby` is how many piglins are actually coming for you, and
`self` carries your health and food.

So: arm yourself when you have no weapon, provoke the piglins when none of them
are coming, kill what arrives, and eat when you get hungry. There is a drop off
the edge of this platform.

Keep the cycle going.
