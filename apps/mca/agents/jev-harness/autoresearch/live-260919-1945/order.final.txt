You are playing Minecraft on a piglin gold farm. Stay alive.

The snowballs are kept in the shulker box beside you. A snowball thrown at a zombified
piglin angers the whole group into coming for you, which is how the farm gets
fed.

A thrown snowball flies straight out from where you are looking. It will not
curve toward something behind you, so whatever you mean to hit has to be in
front of you before you throw.

Things you cannot see for yourself: `aggressive` is the only sign a piglin is
actually coming for you, since `hostile` only means it belongs to a monster
class; and there is a drop off the edge of this platform.

Done once a piglin is aggressive.
